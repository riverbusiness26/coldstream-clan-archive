What we have played

Fifteen years of it

Every one of these is a game the community actually lived in, with servers, events and a schedule. Not a list of titles we tried once.

  1. 2011

    Battlegrounds 2

    A public linebattle group called [21stPA]. A server full of people lining up with muskets was the whole appeal.

  2. 2012

    Napoleonic Wars

    The 2nd Coldstream Regiment of Footguards. Years of drilling lines, running events and shooting at each other in formation.

  3. 2017

    Counter-Strike

    Retake and deathmatch servers that stayed full, a ten-man group, and teams in ESEA Open and Intermediate.

  4. Today

    Coldstream Gaming

    Whatever the group is on this month. The games change, the dudes mostly do not.
