HISTORY



The origins of the coldstream guard lies in the English Civil War, in which Oliver Cromwell gave Colonel George Monck permission to form his own regiment as part of the New Model Army. This regiment served in the battle of Dunbar. The famous victory in which the round heads defeated the forces of Charles Stuart. After Cromwell's death, Monck help with the restoration of the Monarch. For his services he  was rewarded  The Order of the Garter and his regiment was assigned to keep order in London. However, the new parliament soon ordered his regiment to be disbanded with the other regiments of the New Model Army, but after succesfully defeating  the rebellion by Fifth Monarchist led by Thomas Venner. They became the  Royal Regiment of The Lord General's Regiment of Foot Guards. Nine years later with the death of Monck, The Earl of Craven took command and the regiment became known as The Coldstream Regiment of Foot Guards.
