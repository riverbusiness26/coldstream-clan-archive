What is the Seventh Coalition?

The Seventh Coalition is a social group that was formed on July 10 2015 by the following regiments: 2nd (Coldstream) Regiment of Foot Guards, 8th (The King's) Regiment of Foot, 22nd (Cheshire) Regiment of Foot, 42nd (Royal Highlanders) Regiment of Foot, and the 87th (Royal Irish Fusiliers) Regiment of Foot. Our mission is to bring like-minded regiments together to revive some old values, promote friendly relations and social activities, free from toxicity. We strive to keep traditions and values from the past alive in the events we participate in, as well as in other activities.

Such traditions and values are:
    • Being courteous and showing respect for one another
    • Coordinating and cooperating in events
    • Matches between regiments are friendly and not the product of serious rivalries
    • Remaining professional at all times, whether you are wronged by someone else or disciplining someone
    • Support longstanding events
    • Work with other organizations to facilitate the maintenance of a better community
    Historically, the Seventh Coalition was an alliance that was formed by the nations of Austria, The United Kingdom, Prussia and Russia in 1815 to end the warmongering ways of Napoleon Bonaparte. Officially formed at the Congress of Vienna, the Seventh Coalition's creation was witnessed by all other participants of the congress and most eventually joined the Coalition.
     

    Member Regiments

    		2nd (Coldstream) Regiment of Foot Guards
     
    The Coldstream Regiment of Footguards was founded in Mount & Musket, a modification for Mount & Blade:Warband after a long streak of multiple trial and error regiments lead by founder and current leader Crawford in Battlegrounds 2. In both Mount and Musket and Napoleonic Wars, the Coldstream has striven to be one of the most disciplined yet enjoyable regiments in the community. We believe that fun and discipline are in no way mutually exclusive. We embrace discipline not because we are told to, but because we understand the need to act in a coordinated manner.

    8th (Kings) Regiment of Foot
     
    The 8th (The King's) Regiment Of Foot was founded in 2010 by the uncle of the current leader and was later taken over by Jorge six months after its creation. Jorge has commanded the 8th for over 4 years now. The 8th strives to be a regiment that can have as much fun as possible, but still retain the skill to compete with top-tier regiments. The Seventh Coalition is a new chapter in 8th history and will hopefully be one filled with great melee charges.

    22nd (Cheshire) Regiment of Foot
     
    The 22nd (Cheshire) Regiment of Foot is a line infantry regiment that was formed in January 2014 out of the remnants of the 44th (East Essex) Regiment of Foot, after it was disbanded. The 22nd is comprised of former 44th members as well as new people that have earned their place as valued members. The officers took it upon themselves to uphold the same values that the 44th (East Essex) Regiment of Foot was founded upon and continue its traditions by forming the 22nd "Red Knights". We pride ourselves on being serious and disciplined, yet light-hearted. Having fun is a huge factor for us and that will never be compromised by overly strict rules or policies. We're looking for people who strive to be in a professional and skilled environment. If you match these qualities, then you will fit in perfectly. We believe that our tenets match those of the Seventh Coalition so that is why we joined it.

    42nd (Royal Highlanders) Regiment of Foot
     
    The 42nd Black Watch is a relatively new regiment to Napoleonic Wars but its core is assembled from longstanding members of the North American community. Although we have a long tradition with the competitive side of the community, we have decided to abstain and assist the many public battles that are available.

    To this end, we have formed the 7th Coalition with other like-minded regiments and are looking forward to new experiences as members of this brigade.

    87th (Royal Irish Fusiliers) Regiment of Foot
     
    The 87th Royal Irish Fusiliers is a Line Infantry unit that focuses on fast paced line formations, crisp volleys and aggressive bayonet charges. The 87th's training regime focuses on bettering combat skills and having fun, but always maintaining discipline within the ranks. The 87th Royal Irish Fusiliers dates back to the days of MM_Prussia_5. This was a mod for Mount & Blade: Warband that had the dodgy experimental artillery and no Russians or Austrians on the field. Once MM_Russia_3 came out the 29th Horse Artillery was born as a precursor to the 87th. The regiment grew and a few months later was rebranded as the 87th Royal Irish Fusiliers due to the increase in members. Over the next four years the 87th expanded greatly. The 87th, as it stands now, is a regiment of tradition: it fields a large dedicated group of soldiers who, at a moments notice, are ready to take up the musket. The 87th sought out other, older regiments and those of like mind to bring together the idea of the Seventh Coalition; which was the brainchild of all five founding regiments.

    2nd Sibiriskii Grenaderski Guards

    Formed in March 2015 as the Two Seasons Gaming Clan and Re-Named in June to the 2nd Sibiriskii Grenaderski Guards. With dedicated members, the 2ySG strive to be a unique and skilled regiment in the Napoleonic Wars community. the 2ySG is an American based regiment with the flag of the Russian Kingdom under their name. They are mainly Line Infantry, but occasionally play cavalry to seek adventures and new experiences on the battlefield. They use maneuvers like those used in the 1800's and rely on skill to dominate their opponents. They are a relaxed and focused regiment that enjoys playing Mount & Blade Warband as well as other games.

    How To Become A Member Regiment

    NOTE: We do not generally accept French regiments into the coalition. However, if there is one regiment that fully embraces our tenets and is wanting to join, we may accept them after a review by all member regiments.

    Before your regiment can become a full member of the Seventh Coalition, it must first sign-up to become a trial regiment. Trial regiments are monitored by full members for the span of one month to ensure that the regiment is a good fit for the Seventh Coalition. Trial regiments are expected to contribute to the same degree as full member regiments to show that they are committed to being productive members of the Seventh Coalition. Trial members will be afforded access to the private Seventh Coalition Staff Steam group to ensure that they are able to participate to the fullest.

    Upon completing the one month probationary period, a trial regiment will be made a full member of the Seventh Coalition and have the same privileges of the other members extended to them. These privileges include a regiment profile space on the Seventh Coalition thread as well as a permanent section of the Seventh Coalition TeamSpeak server. The leader of the regiment will also receive the group officer position on the Seventh Coalition's Steam Group.

    To join, contact one of the following people: [2ndCS] Crawford, 8th Jorge, [22nd] Amit, [42nd] Rothgar, 87th POTUS, or any of the staff on the Seventh Coalition Steam group.
