Coldstream Public Teamspeak


I remember when most of the community relied on ts.22nd.eu (the Official teamspeak for Mount & Musket and early NW) It was amazing having everyone so close, I loved the convenience of messaging other regiment leaders, mercing and just hanging about with new people everyday without hopping from teamspeak to teamspeak. I'd like to bring this experience back for the Napoleonic Wars community, I feel the community has taken a turn in the wrong direction. When I browse the NA forums I see nothing but shitposting, negativity between regiments, arguments about officer aiming, double reggers and all sorts of bullshit. I'm not comfortable with that being the face of the North American community, I want to build a friendly, no bullshit, no drama and just chill environment for all regiments interested in taking part. If this sounds like something you're interested in, copy the template below and reply to this thread with it filled out. If you don't feel comfortable putting it out in public, you may PM me.
