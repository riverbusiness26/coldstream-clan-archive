4v4 Regimental Melee Tournament


Hello! This tournament will be a North American 4v4 groupfighting style melee tournament, hosted by the 26e partnered with Fibre Servers. The event will take place on Sunday, January 19th, 2014 at 5:00 pm EST. Tournaments have been a fun and entertaining activity in this community for a long time and we are happy to finally host one ourselves. The regiment with the winning team will be awarded a game server for any of Fibre servers provided games, which can be found here. The server will be provided for six months free of charge.


Rules

 - All members of a team must be a part of the same regiment.
 - Trash talk should be friendly and playful, not antagonizing.
 - 'Pub' teams will be accepted, but all members must be pubs.
 - Everyone must respect their opponents and the admins.
 - Bayonets only and of course, no shooting.
 - Two teams per regiment max.
 - Follow the rules and enjoy yourself.
