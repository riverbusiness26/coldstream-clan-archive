North American Duel League - 2nd Season- [Colonys22 victor]
« on: November 25, 2013, 03:21:19 am »


After the first season we decided to change the format of this tournament in order to ensure all duels are fought and to make this more fun for everyone. The format will be that of the European Football Champions League. Players will be randomly assigned into groups of 4 players each and then have to fight each of the people in their group once. The best two players of each group will continue to the knock-out stages. From then it will be knock-outs all the way to the final.

Rules:
1. Be respectful! Even if you may have been insulted leave the punishments to the organizers. Sent them evidence in form of screenshots or videos. They will then decide how to punish the offenders.
2. Line infantry only! No guards, sailors, officers or any other type of unit that differs from standard line infantry.
3. Melee speed will be on "Fastest"
4. The duels will be first to 4 during the group stage and first to 6 during the knock-out phase.
5. For the group stage: Win = 3 points, Loss = 0 Points
6. A maximum of 40 players will be allowed into the tournament at first and possibly later expanded.
7. There will be groups of 4 players and the number of groups will vary depending on how many people sign up.
8. Each contestant has to face each other opponent in his group once!
9. The players who come in first and second in their group will advance to the knock-out stages. To determine who comes first and second, the points will be used and in the case of a draw, the rounds won and lost decide. If still drawn the person who won the duel against the person he draws with in the group, will advance.
10. After the group stage, the first from group A will be matched with the second of group B, the first of group B with the second of group A, the first of group C with the second of group D and so on. Then the knock-out stages will continue throughout the tournament until the grande finale. The winner of the final will be crowned Champion.
10. The group matches will be fought on one day and the knock-out stage matches on another (The dates for this tournament will be released once sign-ups open).
11. The 12th_ES server will be used for the tournament.
12. No shows are rated as forfeits and can get you banned from this tournament for the future. So make sure you can actually attend or excuse yourself in advance so that reserves can fill in for you.
13. Sign-ups open on the 1st of December 2013!

How to sign up:
You have to sign up on this thread with the following format: 
Community Name:
Steam Name (Links only):

Referee Sign up!
We will need a couple referees since we want to have multiple duels simultaneously. So Referee Applications are more than welcome. Apply using this format:

Why would you make a good referee?:
Admining experience?:

Those who are referees are still allowed to participate, just not allowed to referee their own matches.

You are only accepted into the tournament once you have received my steam invitation and accepted it!

If you have problems signing up on the forums you can also contact me on steam and sign up that way, but that is only a last resort. My steam name is: [12th] Zzehth

You can also turn to my co-organizers for help or to complain.
