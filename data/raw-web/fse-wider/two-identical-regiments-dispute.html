Forum threads are the only things that actually matter, if your thread was made before theirs it is well within your rights to ask them to change, if they don't comply it is up to the admins and community to enforce this in any way they can. It would however have been a lot easier if you had contacted them before they started attending "official"(whatever that means) events and tried to come to an agreement then and there, to them it may seem that you had essentially given them your go ahead with your silence, making you a bit of a "twat" if you suddenly decide to barge in telling them to shove off. Wether or not they had a steam group first doesn't matter one bit, having an active thread is the deciding factor.

My best advice would be to try and reason with them in as much of a friendly manner as possible, be gentle and helpful.
In this situation it is probably best for you to ask them to have "sufficiently"(In this case it is you who decide what sufficient is) different tags. If that proves undesirable then try to suggest something else, in the interest of being well mannered I'd suggest trying to help make the transition as smooth as possible. They are probably quite new to this and might appreciate the help and advice.

If they act like arrogant and stubborn cockends during the whole procedure then feel free to screw them in any way you see fit.
