Player of the Month System

By Crawford
- Posted Jun 24, 12
Howdy all Midnight Merc Community Gamers,
 
As some of you might have read the title, of course. We'll be setting up a new "Player of the Month" System. The last day of the month, the main Administrators will meet up and discuss who's been the most Loyal, Active and outgoing player within the Midnight Mercs Community. Now, you may be thinking to yourself, How might I get to be the Player of the Month and or What will I get when I am? That's a good question, You recieve "Player of the Month" for doing the following, frequently and in a good manner.
 
 
- Recruiting as much as possible
 
- Make an attempt to populate our servers every once and awhile
 
- Stay active on the forums
 
- Attend events frequently
 
- Be a good sport, and help out noobies as much as possible
 
- Upload screenshots in our gallery
 
- Upload some youtube videos of some Clan mates and you
 
 
All of the listed things are very simple, yet effective. Put some effort in to doing all of the following, and I can assure you one day you'll recieve player of the month. Prizes will be thought of via the Staff team as soon as possible.
 
 
Sincerely,
Crawford
