Website Revival, New Ranking System & Recruitment

By RiveR a - Posted Mar 18, 15

Hello everyone,

It's been a while since I've posted a news post or anything on the website for that matter. Before we get into this I'd like to apologize for my shitty format and writing in general. I don't have any experience as a writer or any of that jazz. I'm just doing this solely to inform you guys what we're planning and what we're working on. Now that we've got that out of the way lets get down to the announcements. So first and foremost as you guys already know we've brought back the site and we're looking to come back in full force. With that being said, I'd like you guys to share as much as possible with the rest of the community what you did today in your game or anything that you'd think others would find interesting.

New Ranking System

We've created some fictional ranks for new members to progress through using a point system, which is still being worked on. We'll need your creative minds to help us think of how the points should be distributed, earned and a way for bonus "points" to be earned. We will be having a community meeting discussing more on this matter at the date posted below. I highly suggest you attend if you'd like your input to be considered.

Community Discussion:  Saturday @ 3:00PM Central / 4:00PM Eastern 3/21/15

Recruitment

Now that our site is back and I've regained the motivation to push the community forward. We need to start bringing in some new faces. You guys don't necessarily have to go out of your way to recruit new members but I would like you guys to at least try to recruit new or current friends if possible. The more the merrier, nobody likes playing alone. At least I know I don't so lets give those lonely gamers a home. If this is not your approach to recruiting then speak to an admin and we'll give you more ideas on how to recruit.
